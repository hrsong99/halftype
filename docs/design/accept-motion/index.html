Accept motion: what happens when Tab takes words, in other apps

John: "I don't see [the glint]… I want to see the realistic animations we could have for when we accept a suggestion in most apps." Today the glint plays only in our own window (Try It). In Slack, Mail, Notes and the rest nothing moves. Every option here can be drawn by our click-through panel on top of the host's own text, at the spot where the ghost text was. We can't recolour the host's text. All we can do is paint over it.

Recommendation: D, the underline sweep, or C, ink settle, if you want something quieter. D never redraws letters, so it can't look doubled when the host's font or zoom is slightly off from ours. That mismatch is the likely reason the glint was kept to our own window. C is the most "native" feel, since the grey just firms up into ink, but it draws our copy of the letters and needs the same alignment care as B. B (today's glint, turned on everywhere) is the one-line change, but it has that same alignment risk. Every option stays at 250 ms or less, so rapid Tabs never pile up, and none of them move or resize anything.
