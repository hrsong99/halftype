Onboarding: Arc / Dia delight, and a seamless Accessibility step

John: “i want a delightful onboarding experience like from arc or dia… and a really seamless one (like for accessibility, I see a lot of apps have an overlay where you can drag the app into accessibility and stuff, do we need that?)… our default is Gemma 4, and we can start downloading the moment the app opens.” A is what ships. B–F are animated HTML options on one clock. G–I are Codex image ideas.

Recommendation: C’s four steps, with D’s docked guide for Accessibility and E’s practice field for step 3.

The welcome sentence writes itself and the ending shows up as a suggestion, so the product explains itself in the first two seconds, the way Dia’s opening does. The Accessibility step opens System Settings with a small card docked beside it that says which switch to flip. When macOS reports the grant (the app already listens for it), Settings closes and Halftype comes back to the front. Gemma 4 E2B is 3.4 GB, so the download often outlasts the onboarding. The practice field runs on scripted suggestions until it finishes, so nobody is left watching a progress bar. You don't need the drag-in icon: asking once puts Halftype in the list, so flipping one switch is enough. The icon only earns its place for a build that isn't in the list, so in D it's a fallback.
