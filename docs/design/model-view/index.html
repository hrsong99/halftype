Model view: how to see what the model sees when it suggests

John: "I'm kind of curious what kind of context is in the model when it's suggesting… a toggle where I can see the information hovering over the suggestion, or a keyboard shortcut that I can press to see what it's seeing and why it's predicting its next tokens." Every mock shows the same invented moment: a chat, you've typed Sounds good, see you tomo, and the ghost reads rrow at the. The numbers are real for this rule set. The first word is 89%, which clears the Gate's 50%. The running total drops to 37% at "station", just under the e^-1 floor, so the ghost stops at "the".

Recommendation: B, the inspector panel, opened from the menu bar. Everything it shows (context, prompt, the held-back word, each token's probability, why it stopped) already exists in the code, so the engine doesn't change. It never covers the text you're typing, and it can't be opened by accident. C (hold a key to peek) is the more fun option if you want it right at the caret, but it needs a key combination that never clashes with typing. D is the cheapest way to see "why" without the context. F looks the coolest but costs the most. Whichever you pick, it stays display-only: nothing is logged or saved, and it's off until you open it.
