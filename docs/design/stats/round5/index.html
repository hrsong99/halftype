Stats, round 5: C with fewer lines

John: “C looks good but there's so many horizontal lines everywhere? Show me some improved designs of C (inc different designs for the other stats).” C had five lines: under the figures, the chart’s top gridline, its baseline, the line over the footer, and the segmented control’s outline. Same real week (Nov 9–10). Hover the bars.

Recommendation: C, the other stats as one quiet line under the sentence, and the chart with only its baseline.

It’s the sentence’s natural follow-on (“831 words · 1 in 8 keys by Tab · 939 Tabs”), adds no shape and needs no line to separate it. Space does the separating. The chart’s top gridline and “24 min” label go: hover gives each day’s number, and the baseline alone keeps the bars grounded. The trade-off: the three stats get smaller, so they read as detail, which is what they are next to the time. If you want them to keep weight, D (label above, no lines) is the next best.
