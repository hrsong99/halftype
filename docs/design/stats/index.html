Halftype’s Stats page, upgraded

What’s being decided: how the Stats page should look, borrowing from Divan Raj’s sleep dashboard you bookmarked. Your note: “(1) hover a bar to see that day’s numbers; (2) a small ‘+12 vs yesterday’ delta next to the headline number; (3) a Day / Week / Month switch, only once there are months of data. Skip the stacked colour bars.”
Found while reading the code: (1) already ships. Hovering a bar today darkens it and changes the grey caption above the chart (Stats uses chartXSelection, and the app targets macOS 26). So B moves that day’s numbers to the bar itself. And there is no hourly data, so a “Day” view can’t exist without new counting: E is Week / Month / All.
Numbers: today (Sun Nov 8) is this Mac’s real stats.json, its only day so far: 189 Tabs, 770 letters, 102 of 2,328 suggestions taken, 481 keys saved of 4,329 typed. The 13 days before are sample data shaped like it (sample-tally.json, odd dates with the personal model, as Compare does). Hover the bars.

Recommendation: C, “your typing, by Tab”, with B’s tooltip.

The one number that says whether Halftype is worth it is the share of your typing Tab did (11% today), not the count of Tabs: 189 Tabs means nothing until you know it’s 1 key in 9. That share is also what the Personal Model experiment is about, so C colours each day’s bar by whether the model was on, and the comparison stops being a separate block under the fold. One series, two honest colours, which is the part of the shot worth taking. B is the smallest change (an afternoon) if you want to keep Tabs as the headline. D is the prettiest and most on-brand (the sky’s dots), but a calendar of 14 days is mostly empty for months. Leave E for when there are months of data, as you said.
