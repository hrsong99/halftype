Stats, round 3: show the value

John: “I think the stats page needs an upgrade to directly show our value. I think we should make it more understandable and intuitive how many tabs they've accepted and letters/words got accepted, along with how much time they saved and stuff. What are the main stats that are most important you think?” All options use this Mac’s real week so far (Mon Nov 9 and Tue Nov 10, counts only, from stats.json), drawn on main’s window. Hover the bars.

Recommendation: B (time saved first), with C’s “1 in every 8 keys” as the share’s label.

The stats that show value, most important first: 1. Time saved (the one people care about; nobody feels “4,168 keys”). 2. Words typed for you (letters ÷ 5, the standard typing-test word, so it’s a feel-able unit). 3. Share of your typing, said as “1 in every 8 keys” not “13%”. 4. Tabs, demoted: it counts effort, not gain. Leave out suggestions taken ÷ shown: it measures the model, not the user’s gain, and it was miscounted until Nov 10.

Time saved needs a typing speed. The mockups assume 40 wpm (200 keys a minute), which is a guess, and “every number says how it was measured”. Better: count the seconds spent actually typing (gaps under 2 s between keys) per day, a count like the others, and divide. Then it’s “21 min at your speed, 52 wpm”. That’s a one-field addition to Tally.Day.
