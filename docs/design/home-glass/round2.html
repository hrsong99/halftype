Home in glass, round 2: C + D, and suggestions that gleam

John: “What about C + D (and can we make the suggestions gleam like that)”. So: cream and ink with the glass ribbon behind (C + D), the suggestion in glass, and ways for it to catch the light. Every window plays the same 6-second loop: the suggestion appears at 0.5 s, you press Tab at 3.8 s and the glass turns to ink.

Recommendation: A, a sweep. One glint crosses the suggestion as it arrives and another as Tab sets it in ink, then it holds still while you read. It's the icon's highlight, it marks the two moments that matter, and it never moves while you're deciding. B's endless flow is pretty but asks for attention the whole time a suggestion is up; C (written in) adds a delay before you can read it. Of the ribbons, D (sharper, like a real glass object) is the most like the icon; E (faint) is the calmest.

Can it gleam? In this window, yes: SwiftUI can mask a moving highlight onto the text. In other apps the suggestion is our panel over their text, and it can gleam too, but it stays grey: a glass gradient won't read on every background.
