The home window, in the icon's materials

John: “we recently came up with a new, glassy icon for halftype right? Can you show me some variants of the home page that takes inspiration from the icon style?” Yes: 5A, a cursive h with a glossy ink stem and an arch that turns to frosted glass, pale blue to peach, on cream. A is today. B–G borrow from it. H–J are Codex image ideas.

Recommendation: C, half ink and half glass. The icon's idea is the app's job: what you wrote is ink, and what Halftype offers is glass until you take it. C makes the window say that, with the suggestion drawn in the icon's glass and your words in its ink, on the icon's cream. It's a SwiftUI gradient on the ghost text in this window, plus the colours. B is the same window without the glass words, if C feels like too much. Drawn suggestions in other apps stay grey either way: the ghost panel has to read on any background.
