Learns your words

Trained on your own messages, so it suggests the names, phrases and mix of Korean and English you actually use.

Retrained routinely

Every few weeks we retrain it on what you've typed since, on our GPUs, so it keeps up with how you write now. That ongoing training is what the subscription pays for. New base models come too.

Your text, handled carefully

With Pro on, Halftype keeps your typing on your Mac and sends it only to train your model; it's deleted on our side when training ends. The model lives on your Mac. Pro off: nothing is saved or sent.

The plan cards are a choice: clicking one changes the button ("Notify Me: Monthly / Yearly / Lifetime"), so each sign-up records which plan they'd buy. The privacy card describes Pro as planned; it goes live only with the privacy policy.
