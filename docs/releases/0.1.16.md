- **Suggestions stay put as you type through them** in apps built on Chrome, like T3 Code, and in web editors. Typing a space the suggestion predicted used to make it disappear and come back, sometimes as a different suggestion. Now it holds, and suggestions right after a space are better too.
- **Suggestions look like the text around them on web pages.** Next to monospaced text, the suggestion is monospaced too, and a suggestion that wraps follows the page's line spacing.
- **The right size and place in Safari.** Where a page spaced its lines out, suggestions were drawn too big and too high. They now match the text, at any zoom.
