- **See what the model sees.** Turn on Show What the Model Sees in Settings › General, then tap ⌃⌥ while you type: a card above each suggestion shows the text the model was given (from the screen and from your field), the half-typed word it continued, and how likely it found each word. Tap ⌃⌥⇧ for the whole prompt, and ⌃⌥ again to hide it. It's off by default, and what it shows is never saved.
