- **More suggestions, by default.** Halftype now shows its best guess whenever it has one, and a longer one, so Tab saves more of your typing. More of what it shows is wrong; keep typing and it goes away. In replays of English and Korean messages it saved about 10 more keys in every 100 than before ([how it was measured](https://github.com/hrsong99/halftype/blob/main/docs/measuring.md#the-defaults-since-2026-11-10-long-always-and-a-two-key-minimum)). If you changed these settings yourself, your choice is kept.
- **Two clearer settings.** General's How Often is now **Suggestions**: Always, When Likely, or When Sure (the old cautious default). Length is Long or Short. Less and Medium are gone; if you had picked one, you get When Sure or Long.
- The Tab Motion preview in General plays only while the window is in front.
