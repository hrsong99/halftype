- **Gemma 4 E2B is the default model** for new installs. If you already have a model, Halftype keeps it instead of downloading 3.4 GB.
- **A short guided setup on first launch:** grant Accessibility, then practise Tab, Shift-Tab and Esc in Halftype's own window.
- **Start at login** is now on by default at the end of setup.
- **Tab shows a short trail in other apps**, at the text you just took. Pick Trail, Flash or Off in Settings.
- **Closing brackets and quotes stay with the suggested word**, so you get `this)` and `"this"`, not just `this`.
- The menu-bar dot that marks something new is red, like other macOS badges.
- The Stats page no longer overcounts how many suggestions were shown.
