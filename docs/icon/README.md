# The icon

Three options from Codex's built-in image tool. `generate.sh` sends `prompts.txt`; `build.sh` masks them onto the macOS icon grid and builds `Resources/`.

`option-d` is the icon since the rename to Halftype (2026-11-08): a cursive h, half ink and half glass, from logo round 5 (`docs/design/logo/`, option 5A), cropped to centre it. `option-a`, a caret then what hasn't been typed yet, was the icon before.

- **a, the first icon.** It draws what the app does, in any language. In [options-compared.png](options-compared.png), its bright bar on a dark ground is the only mark still crisp at 16 px.
- **b, ring, caret, faded ring.** Reads as `ㅇ|ㅇ` to a Korean eye and `O|O` to anyone else. The pale ground melts into a light window, and the faded ring is gone at 16 px.
- **c, 잇 with a faded ㅅ.** The best idea, and correct Hangul: the syllable isn't finished yet. But it says nothing to someone who doesn't read Korean, and it's mush at 16 px.

The menu-bar glyph, `menubar.svg`, is the same h drawn by hand, its arch at 38% opacity like the glass, because a template image has to be black and alpha at exact pixel sizes.
