A full landing page design for "Halftype", a Mac menu-bar app that shows grey inline typing suggestions in Korean and English from a local AI model. Tall 2:3 vertical web page mockup, desktop width. Direction: Korean risograph print poster. Off-white paper, two spot inks only (cobalt blue and peach orange) printed as coarse halftone dots with slight misregistration. Huge hero line in Korean "반만 치세요" with the second half of a sentence printed as light grey ghost text after a blinking text caret, and a small keycap labelled "Tab". English pitch below: "You type half. It writes the rest." A pill button "Download for Mac" and a small monospace box "brew install --cask halftype". Below, three sections as print panels: the keys Tab / Shift-Tab / Esc, "Nothing you type leaves your Mac", Korean and English side by side. The reference image is the real app window (a rounded grey text field with a model pill and power button); include that field in the hero. Crisp legible text, no people, no fake logos.
